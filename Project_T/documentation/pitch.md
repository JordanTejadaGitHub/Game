# Pitch and Store Page

Phase 4, topic 13 of `design_plan.md`. The pitch is also a filter: **if a feature doesn't support
the hook, question it.**

## The hook

**Your towers are the maze, and nobody gets hurt.**

Two things no competitor combines:
1. **Maze-building** tower defense: every Warden is a wall, and creatures re-route around what you
   build. You design the path, not just the defence.
2. **Cozy and non-violent**: creatures aren't enemies, they're lost. Wardens *soothe* the Blight
   away, colour floods back, and the creature hops home.

Everything else (roguelite Dreams, evolving Wardens, the Memory Grove) supports those two.

| | Line | Leans on |
|---|---|---|
| **A** *(recommended)* | *A cozy maze-building tower defense roguelite. Grow a living maze of Wardens, soothe the Blight, and help every lost creature find its way home.* | clarity: genre + fantasy |
| B | *Grow a living maze. Soothe the Blight. Nobody gets hurt.* | punchy tagline, needs genre elsewhere |
| C | *Tower defense where your towers are the maze, and the "enemies" are just lost.* | the twist, slightly jokey |

Use B as the trailer/capsule tagline and A as the store description's first line.

## Steam short description (≤ 300 characters)

> A cozy maze-building tower defense roguelite. Plant Wardens that become the walls of a winding
> forest path, soothe blighted creatures back to colour, and dream up new combos every run. A new
> forest every time; nobody ever gets hurt.

## Key selling points (store page, in this order)

1. **Your towers are the maze.** Every Warden is a wall. Build the path, watch creatures re-route
   live, and make the long way round the only way round.
2. **Nothing dies.** Creatures are cleansed, not defeated: the grey drains away, their colour comes
   back, and they scurry home happy.
3. **Grow and combine.** Sprouts evolve into 6 Warden families and their branches. Soak the path
   with Rain Lilies, then chain lightning through every damp creature.
4. **A new forest every run.** Winding ridges, withered trees to tend, and a different Dream each
   time you play.
5. **Remember and grow.** Seeds from every run grow your Memory Grove, unlock new Wardens, and
   slowly reveal the story of the Hollow.

## Steam tags (first 5 matter most)

Tower Defense, Roguelite, Cozy, Strategy, Relaxing, then: Pixel Graphics, Wholesome, Cute, Nature,
Procedural Generation, Replay Value, Singleplayer, Steam Deck (once verified).

## Capsule art concept

The capsule must read at tiny sizes (231×87), so **one idea, big shapes**:

- **Composition:** a winding path seen from above, lined with Wardens. A **Sporeling** (the purple
  golem from the concept art) large in the foreground, mid-puff.
- **The idea in one image:** the creatures on the path are **grey before the Sporeling and full
  colour after it**. Grey-to-colour is the game's core visual; it should be the capsule's too.
- **Top:** the Heartwood's canopy glowing softly; the logo sits in the leaves.
- **Palette:** warm greens and golds against the cool grey Blight.

## Trailer: the first 10 seconds

Most viewers decide in the first 5 seconds. Show both hooks immediately; no logo intro.

1. **0–3 s:** close-up of a grey Leaf Bug walking. A spore puff hits it; colour floods back; it
   hops off. (Hook 2)
2. **3–7 s:** pull back to a winding maze of Wardens. A new Warden drops onto the path, and the
   route line snaps into a longer detour live. (Hook 1)
3. **7–10 s:** tagline *"Grow a living maze. Soothe the Blight. Nobody gets hurt."*, then a quick
   montage (lightning chaining through damp creatures, a boss turning to colour, the Grove).

## Screenshots (first 5)

## Positioning

- **For players of:** maze tower defense and TD roguelites (e.g. Rogue Tower) and cozy strategy
  (e.g. Dorfromantik, Islanders, Thronefall).
- **Short version for press and Next Fest:** *"a maze tower defense roguelite, but cozy"*.
- **Platforms:** PC (Steam), Steam Deck verified as a goal (controller support needed; see
  `game_design.md` release prep).
- **Price:** typical for this genre and scope is $9.99–$19.99; decide closer to launch based on
  content (runs to finish, number of Wardens, biomes).

## Title

"The Heartwood Remembers" is the working title. Before the store page goes up:
- **Check it's free:** search Steam, itch.io and the trademark databases for "Heartwood" (a common
  word in games and fantasy).
- **Test it for search:** a title that hints at the genre helps (people search "tower defense").

Alternatives to consider: **Wardens of the Heartwood**, **Blightwood**, **The Gentle Maze**,
**Heartwood: A Cozy Maze Defense** (subtitle carries the genre).

## What this means for the game

These moments are the marketing; make sure they're great early:
- **The cleanse** (animation, sound, colour) needs to feel wonderful; it's in every trailer shot.
- **The live re-route** when placing a Warden must read instantly (route line, preview).
- **The Sporeling** is the mascot; it should look its best and appear in the first run.
- **Grey vs colour** must be obvious even in a small screenshot.
