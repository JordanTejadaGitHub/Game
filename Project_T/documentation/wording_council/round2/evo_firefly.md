Problems that run across rows:
- **"flare" is never defined.** The word first appears in the Strung Lights tip ("Not ... flares") and in Far Lights, with no
  explanation. It means one damage-only hit from that Firefly (class_kit.calling_hit: at most one second of its damage). Define it
  where it first appears in each line: Far Lights and Marsh Lights, the two follow-ups that open the flare rows.
- **"Bosses take half" is said on some rows and not others.** Every flare passes BOSS_SHARE (firefly_lights.gd:1064, 1080) and
  every jump halves on a boss (firefly_lights.gd:2197). The tips say so on Lantern Road, Parting Light, Circling Light,
  Show the Way and Constellation. They don't on Far Lights, Marsh Lights, Drawn In, Borrowed Light or Sightline.
- **"Not a crit for other cards"** (Bonfire, Moth to Flame, Pyre) is a fragment that is hard to read.
  Use "Other cards don't count it as a crit."
- **"glow" means two things.** In "Overlapping glows are one glow" (Strung Lights) a glow is the merged light. In
  "2 Fireflies' glows overlap" (Bonfire, Pyre) it is one Firefly's own glow. Keep "glow" for one Firefly's area; the Strung Lights
  tip says "count as one" instead.
